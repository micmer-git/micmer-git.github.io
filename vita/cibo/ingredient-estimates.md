## How to read this log

Repeated angles taken close together are treated as one food event. Store displays, menus, scenery and meals clearly belonging to other people are not counted as personal intake unless the photographed plate appears to be the subject of the food log.

Ingredient wording distinguishes what is visible from what is inferred:

- **Visible:** identifiable directly in the image.
- **Likely:** customary ingredient strongly suggested by appearance.
- **Possible:** plausible but not reliably distinguishable from the photo.
- Nutrition is shown as **kcal | protein / carbohydrate / fat | fibre** for the pictured serving. Restaurant oil, cheese, sauces and the fraction actually eaten are the largest uncertainties.
- Estimates assume the whole plated serving was eaten. Shared dishes, tasting menus and multi-person tables may substantially overstate personal consumption.

## Overall ingredient pattern

Most frequently recurring visible or strongly implied ingredients:

- **Legumes:** lentils, chickpeas and beans.
- **Grains/starches:** pasta, rice, polenta, oats, dark bread, pizza dough, potatoes and sweet potatoes.
- **Vegetables:** tomatoes, leafy greens/rocket, onions, carrots, pumpkin/squash, peppers, green beans and mixed roasted vegetables.
- **Protein foods:** eggs, cheese, yoghurt, legumes, occasional beef/pork, chicken and fish.
- **Added fats:** olive oil predominates in likely home/Italian-style dishes; butter, cream and cheese are more likely in risotto, pastry, quiche and restaurant food.
- **Treats/fuel:** cake, fruit tarts, croissants, café pastries, jam, chocolate desserts and cycling snacks.

The photographed pattern is generally rich in vegetables, legumes, whole grains and fibre. The greatest calorie uncertainty comes from olive oil, cheese, pastry, creamy sauces, restaurant portions and shared celebration tables. Because many days have only one photographed meal, these images cannot support a reliable estimate of total daily energy intake.
