The shape of a year

How the times are figured. Ascents were recorded by Michele Merelli on the Strava segment “Gazzaniga-Orezzo cimitero” (3.36 km, 282 m). The full Gazzaniga → Orezzo climb is 4.24 km — about 26% longer. With “Full climb” selected, each time is scaled by ×1.262 (the distance ratio, at constant average speed); “As recorded” shows the untouched 3.36 km times. Speed, heart rate, power and VAM are always as recorded. The route, elevation and tornanti come from a 2022 GPS ride of the climb.
